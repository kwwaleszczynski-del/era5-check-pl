How good is ERA5 in Poland?

Hourly ERA5 and CERRA reanalyses at 19 IMGW-PIB stations, 2010-2015: 10 m wind speed against synoptic observations, global horizontal irradiance against the actinometric network, what the errors do to wind and PV energy estimates, and which long-term correction (MCP) repairs them best.
